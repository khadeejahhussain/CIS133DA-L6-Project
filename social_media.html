Social media websites and apps let people share information, photos, and videos and communicate with others online. Businesses can use social media to introduce their products and services, share updates, and connect with customers.

Social Media Comparison

Facebook

Facebook is a social networking platform where people share posts, photos, and videos. Businesses can use a Facebook Page to introduce their services, share updates, and communicate with customers.

Source: Facebook f logo (2019).svg

Instagram

Instagram is a social media platform for sharing photos and videos. Businesses can use it to showcase products, share their story, and interact with customers through comments and messages.

Source: Instagram logo 2022.svg

LinkedIn

LinkedIn is a professional networking platform where people connect with colleagues and share work-related information. Businesses can use it to introduce their company, share industry updates, and connect with potential employees, clients, and business partners.

Source: LinkedIn Logo 2013 (2).svg
